Fit

One size. Some buyers of this blank say it runs small, or tall on the crown. We have not worn a sample. If you want a slouchy or large-head fit, this may not be the hat.

Care

Hand wash. Acrylic sheds microfibers when washed. Machine-washing can collapse the cuff. Air dry.

Embroidery

The cuff is stretchy, so fine detail gets eaten and the mark can land a little crooked. This is a two-color, low-detail file on purpose. Placement is not guaranteed pixel-perfect.

Manufacturing defects (damage, failed embroidery, bad seams) are replaced or refunded. Because each beanie is made to order, we can't accept returns for change of mind or fit.
