Wrap alignment

The cover is printed flat and then wrapped and bound around the book, so the exact position of the art relative to the spine and edges can shift slightly from copy to copy. The design is full bleed with the title kept well inside the front panel, so a small shift won't cut anything off — but no two copies align identically.
