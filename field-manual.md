## 0. The one-page operating system

Strip the names off the 2026 cases and they collapse to five rules. Everything after this page is how to run them.

**1. Own a list.** A weekly newsletter in a sharp niche is still the connective tissue. beehiiv's first-party *State of Newsletters 2026* says publications launched in 2025 hit a median first dollar in 66 days, a median 482 subscribers in month one, and a median 8,314 by the end of year one. Weekly is the dominant cadence (46.67% of creators). The sister report, *State of Paid Newsletters 2026*, says the typical creator flipped on a paid tier 45 days after starting — a median of people who already charged, not a permission slip. Median free-to-paid conversion is 0.62%. The AI vertical leaks 13.33% of paid subscribers a month. Write the issues before you pick a host. Stay free until paid is a different product.

Sources: [The State of Newsletters 2026, beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-newsletters-2026) · [The State of Paid Newsletters 2026, beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-paid-newsletters-2026)

**2. Ship a portfolio of related things, not a mythic flagship.** On 4 January 2026 Marc Lou published a self-reported "$1,032,000 in 2025." In the same post he said the year was down about 20%, mixed in $147,000 of index-fund returns and $14,339 of X payouts, and wrote that nine of ten TrustMRR verticals failed. Fernando Pessagno's 2026 Indie Hackers writeup is the un-glamorous twin: $500k+ lifetime (self-reported; header more than $15K a month) across three paid design tools and a free funnel, plus a graveyard of forks of the same problem. Hit-rate is the strategy. A portfolio of zombies is a support desk with extra domains.

Sources: [Marc Lou, "I made $1,032,000 in 2025"](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-made-1-032-000-in-2025) · [Fernando Pessagno on Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/getting-fired-and-using-it-as-motivation-to-build-a-successful-niche-portfolio-nabmB9HOZvkx4J9902j6) · [Solopreneurship.eu case lab](https://solopreneurship.eu/articles/case-lab-marc-lou-1m-solo-year/)

**3. Price like a solution.** InsightRaider's June 2026 analysis of 146,271 Gumroad products says the $30–$49 band converts about 28% better than sub-$10 equivalents. Products under $10 are roughly 35% of listings and about 0.8% of platform revenue. 44% of products have generated exactly $0 in lifetime revenue. Tiered products with two or three tiers generate about 2x the revenue of single-price listings. Newsletter default price has not moved since 2024: $10 a month or $100 a year. Business sits at a median $15; investing at $27; travel at $7. Sub-$10 packs and hourly billing are how you work more for less.

Sources: [InsightRaider, How to Price Digital Products on Gumroad in 2026](https://insightraider.com/en/answers/how-to-price-digital-templates) · [beehiiv paid report](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-paid-newsletters-2026) · [Press Gazette, June 2026](https://pressgazette.co.uk/newsletters/newsletters-2026-prices-retention-churn/)

**4. Write the stop-loss before you fall in love.** Zero monthly recurring revenue is a message. Low-but-not-zero after a year of grinding is a drain. A self-reported $12,400 MRR can still be a guilt machine if churn is a treadmill and the number cannot pay the humans. Fast kills (six days, a pre-written $100 rule) and late kills (twelve months, a unit-economics rule that arrived at month nine) are the same skill on different calendars. Half-alive is not a state you are allowed to keep.

Sources: [Pessagno](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/getting-fired-and-using-it-as-motivation-to-build-a-successful-niche-portfolio-nabmB9HOZvkx4J9902j6) · [OperatorBook, killing a SaaS at $12K MRR](https://www.operatorbook.dev/stories/why-we-killed-our-saas-at-12k-mrr) · [Felix Lenhard, Kill or Commit, 20 January 2026](https://felixlenhard.com/blog/kill-or-commit-how-to-decide-on-struggling-projects/)

**5. Disclose first. Then distribute.** July 2026 is when the platforms stopped pretending generic AI was a growth hack. YouTube named three inauthentic buckets and took them out of the Partner Program. Substack handed every reader a scan button and every writer a "How I make this" box. Medium already refuses to paywall AI-generated text, disclosed or not. The Reuters Institute's 2026 Digital News Report put trust in AI-chatbot news answers at 20% globally, against 37% for news overall. On 2 August 2026, Article 50 of the EU AI Act started requiring people to be told when they are talking to a machine. The remaining path for a cited, AI-disclosed brief is X plus an owned email list.

Sources: [TechCrunch, 20 July 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/) · [The Verge, 21 July 2026](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/968855/substack-pangram-ai-detecting-tool) · [Medium AI content policy](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-content-policy) · [Reuters Institute, Digital News Report 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary) · [Article 50, EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/)
