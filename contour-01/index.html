Contour 01

A generative topographic line study. Matte paper poster, printed on demand in the USA.

Because it ships in a tube, expect some paper curl on arrival. Flattening under books for a day or framing it takes care of this. We recommend framing — frames are not included.

The paper

Enhanced matte paper: 10.3 mil thick, 189 g/m², 94% opacity. Water-based inkjet print. Unframed print only.
